Welcome to Your Allergen Training

Food allergies are a serious and growing health concern in Australia. At RaRa Ramen, we handle multiple high-risk allergens daily — from soy and sesame to eggs, wheat, and shellfish. Even a drop or crumb can cause a life-threatening reaction.

Why This Matters Food allergy affects 1 in 10 babies, 1 in 20 children, and 1 in 50 adults in Australia. Most deaths from food allergies happen outside the home — in food service businesses like ours.

By completing this course, you will be able to:

  • Identify Australia's mandatory declarable allergens
  • Distinguish between food allergies and food intolerances
  • Understand your legal obligations under Australian food safety law
  • Prevent cross-contamination in RaRa Ramen's kitchen
  • Communicate allergen information accurately to customers
  • Respond to an allergic reaction or anaphylaxis emergency

Course Structure

This training consists of 5 modules, each with reading material, interactive activities, and a quiz. You must complete all modules and pass each quiz with at least 80% to receive your certificate.

Who Must Complete This Training?

  • All kitchen staff (ramen cooks, prep cooks, kitchen hands)
  • All front-of-house staff (servers, counter staff, managers)
  • Food Safety Supervisors
  • Any new team member before their first shift handling food
Refresher Training All staff must complete refresher training annually, or whenever there is a menu change, new ingredient, or allergen-related incident.
