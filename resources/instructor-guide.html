For Food Safety Supervisors

Instructor Guide & Google Classroom Setup

Purpose: This guide helps Food Safety Supervisors set up, deliver, and track the RaRa Ramen Allergen Safety Training through Google Classroom.

Setting Up in Google Classroom

1
Create a Google Classroom class

Go to classroom.google.com → Click "+" → "Create class"

  • Class name: RaRa Ramen — Allergen Safety Training
  • Section: [Store Name/Location]
  • Subject: Food Safety
2
Upload the training files

Upload the entire RaRa-Ramen-Allergy-Training folder to Google Drive. The HTML files will open directly in any browser.

3
Create assignments for each module

In Google Classroom, create 5 assignments (one per module) + 1 final assessment:

AssignmentAttachPointsDue
Module 1: Introduction to Allergen Awareness module-1.html (from Drive) 10 Set date
Module 2: Australian Allergen Laws & PEAL module-2.html 10 Set date
Module 3: Identifying Allergens at RaRa module-3.html 10 Set date
Module 4: Cross-Contamination Prevention module-4.html 10 Set date
Module 5: Communication & Emergency Response module-5.html 10 Set date
Final Assessment final-assessment.html 50 Set date
4
Add submission instructions

For each module assignment, add these instructions:

"Read the module thoroughly and complete the Knowledge Check questions at the end. When finished, submit a private comment confirming you have completed the module and your score on the Knowledge Check. Then proceed to the next module."

For the Final Assessment:

"Complete the 15-question assessment. You must score at least 80% (12/15) to pass. Take a screenshot of your result and attach it to this assignment. If you do not pass, review the modules and retake the assessment."
5
Invite staff members

Use the class code or email invitations to add all kitchen and front-of-house staff to the Classroom.

Alternative: Convert Final Assessment to Google Forms

For automatic grading, you can recreate the final assessment as a Google Form quiz:

  1. Go to forms.google.com → Create new form
  2. Click the Settings gear → Enable "Make this a quiz"
  3. Add the 15 questions from the final assessment as multiple-choice questions
  4. Set the correct answers and point values (1 point each)
  5. Set passing score to 12/15 (80%)
  6. Attach the Google Form to the Final Assessment assignment in Classroom

Delivery Recommendations

Option A: Self-Paced Online (Recommended)

  • Staff complete modules at their own pace over 1–2 weeks
  • Each module takes approximately 20–25 minutes
  • Total course time: approximately 2 hours including the final assessment
  • Staff can access on any device with a web browser
  • Follow-up with a brief in-person practical session in the kitchen

Option B: Facilitated Group Session

  • Deliver modules as a group training session (2–3 hours)
  • Project modules on a screen and discuss as a team
  • Pause at Knowledge Checks for group discussion
  • Include hands-on demonstrations (handwashing, cleaning, using the matrix)
  • Complete final assessment individually

Option C: Blended

  • Staff complete Modules 1–3 online (theory)
  • Facilitated session for Modules 4–5 (practical demonstrations)
  • Final assessment completed individually

Tracking Completion

Records to Maintain

  • Staff name and position
  • Date started and date completed
  • Final assessment score (must be 80% or higher)
  • Next refresher due date (annual)
  • Trainer/supervisor name who verified completion
Regulatory Requirement Keep training records for all staff. These may be requested during food safety audits or investigations. Records should be maintained for the duration of employment plus 2 years.

When to Require Refresher Training

  • Annually — all staff must complete refresher training every 12 months
  • Menu changes — whenever new items are added or recipes change
  • New ingredients or suppliers — when allergen profiles may have changed
  • After an incident — any allergic reaction or near miss
  • New staff — must complete before their first shift handling food
  • Regulatory updates — when food allergen laws change

Suggested Practical Activities

Supplement the online modules with these hands-on activities:

Activity 1: Allergen Matrix Challenge

Give staff a blank allergen matrix and ask them to fill it in for 5 menu items without looking at the answer. Compare with the completed matrix.

Activity 2: Label Reading

Provide 5–10 food labels (including some with old-style labelling). Ask staff to identify all mandatory allergens present.

Activity 3: Role Play — Allergy Order

One staff member plays the customer (with a specific allergy), another plays front-of-house, and another plays kitchen. Practice the full communication chain from order to service.

Activity 4: Cleaning Demonstration

Demonstrate the difference between cleaning and sanitising. Show correct handwashing technique. Time staff to ensure 20-second wash.

Activity 5: Emergency Drill

Walk through the anaphylaxis response procedure. Practice laying someone flat, locating the emergency response card, and calling 000. (Do NOT use a real EpiPen for practice.)
