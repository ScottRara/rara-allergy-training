Allergen Cleaning Checklist

Instructions: Complete this checklist each shift. Print, laminate, and use with a whiteboard marker for daily use.

Date: _____________    Shift: AM / PM    Completed By: _____________

Before Service

All benches and prep surfaces cleaned with warm water and detergent
All utensils, tongs, ladles washed in hot soapy water or dishwasher
Separate allergen-free utensils (labelled/colour-coded) are clean and in place
Chopping boards washed and sanitised
Storage areas checked — allergen items sealed, labelled, and on correct shelves
Clean cloths and paper towel stocked
Hand wash station stocked with soap and paper towels
Allergen menu matrix displayed and up to date
Emergency response card visible in kitchen

During Service (When Allergy Order Received)

Hands washed with soap and warm water (not just sanitiser)
Clean gloves put on (non-latex)
Prep area wiped down with clean cloth, warm water, and detergent
Clean, separate utensils used for allergen-free preparation
All ingredients checked against allergen matrix / PIFs
All kitchen staff notified that an allergy meal is being prepared
Allergy meal prepared BEFORE other meals (where possible)
Bowl/plate checked for cleanliness — no food residue
Allergy identification card placed with meal
Meal sent out FIRST and confirmed with serving staff

After Service

All surfaces cleaned with warm water and detergent
All equipment disassembled and cleaned (blenders, mixers, whisks)
Storage areas tidied — spills cleaned, containers sealed
Dirty tea towels and cloths sent to laundry (NOT reused)
Any ingredient changes or new stock received noted for matrix update
Any allergen incidents or near misses reported

Reminder: What Does NOT Remove Allergens

  • Sanitiser alone (must clean with detergent first)
  • Hand sanitiser (must wash hands with soap and water)
  • Wiping hands on a tea towel
  • Using a dirty or used cloth
  • Rinsing with water only (need detergent)
