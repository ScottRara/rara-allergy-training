THE 6-STEP CLEANING & SANITISING PROCEDURE
  1. Pre-Clean: Scrape/wipe food scraps off. Rinse with water.
  2. Wash: Hot water (45°C) + detergent. Soak if needed. Scrub.
  3. Rinse: Rinse off detergent and loosened residue.
  4. Sanitise: Apply heat (77°C+) or food-grade chemical sanitiser. Allow contact time.
  5. Final Rinse: Rinse off sanitiser (if manufacturer requires it).
  6. Air Dry: Air dry or use single-use paper towel. NEVER use tea towels.
Sanitising does NOT work on dirty surfaces. ALWAYS clean first!

BLEACH SANITISER DILUTION (10% CHLORINE)
1 mL bleach → 1 Litre COLD water

Contact time: minimum 7 seconds on surface

Use cold water (hot water evaporates chlorine). Always check SDS for specific products.

HANDWASHING PROCEDURE
  1. Wet hands under warm running water
  2. Apply liquid soap (not bar soap)
  3. Scrub 15–30 seconds — palms, between fingers, under nails, backs, thumbs, wrists
  4. Rinse under clean running water
  5. Turn off tap with paper towel or elbow
  6. Dry completely with single-use paper towel
Do:
  • Use dedicated handwash basin
  • Liquid soap dispenser
  • Single-use paper towels
  • 15–30 sec scrub time
Don't:
  • Use hand sanitiser instead
  • Dry on apron or tea towel
  • Use food prep sink
  • Skip drying step
COLOUR-CODED CHOPPING BOARDS
Red — Raw meat
Blue — Raw fish / seafood
Yellow — Raw poultry
Green — Fruits & vegetables
White — Bread / bakery / dairy
Brown — Cooked meat

Clean and sanitise between EVERY use. Dispose of damaged boards immediately.

EQUIPMENT SANITISING FREQUENCY

Equipment touching hazardous food at room temperature:

Every 4 Hours

(Slicers, blenders, stick mixers, prep surfaces)

"Clean as you go" — don't wait until end of service!
