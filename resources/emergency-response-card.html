ALLERGIC REACTION EMERGENCY RESPONSE

RaRa Ramen — Display in Kitchen & Front Counter

MILD / MODERATE SIGNS:

Swelling of lips, face, eyes
Hives or welts
Tingling mouth
Abdominal pain, vomiting

SEVERE SIGNS (ANAPHYLAXIS):

Difficult/noisy breathing
Swelling of tongue
Throat swelling/tightness
Wheeze or persistent cough
Difficulty talking / hoarse voice
Dizziness or collapse
Pale and floppy (young children)

WHAT TO DO:

1
LAY THE PERSON FLAT Do NOT allow them to stand or walk. If breathing is difficult, allow them to sit with legs outstretched.
2
GIVE ADRENALINE (EpiPen) Follow the customer's ASCIA Action Plan if they have one. Administer their adrenaline injector if available. Instructions are on the device.
3
CALL AN AMBULANCE Call Triple Zero immediately. State "anaphylaxis."
CALL 000
4
STAY WITH THE PERSON Monitor breathing. Do not leave them alone until the ambulance arrives.
5
PRESERVE THE FOOD Keep the food the customer was eating. Freeze and label it for investigation.

After the incident: Complete an incident report. Notify the Food Safety Supervisor. Conduct an internal investigation.
